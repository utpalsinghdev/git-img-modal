GitHub Image Preview

Click an image to open the page gallery. Browse with the arrow buttons or ← → keys. The counter shows your position.

Use ↑ to zoom in and ↓ to zoom out. Press Esc or click the backdrop to close. Use 100% / Fit to reset image sizing.

Preloads up to five images in each direction for faster browsing.

Runs on HTTP and HTTPS pages. No image uploads, tracking, or extra permissions.
